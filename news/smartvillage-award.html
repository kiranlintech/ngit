Ngit | Soft Town | Rural IT |Press Release on 01-01-2012

 

March 5th 2015, Chittoor: Narayani GhramIT Systems Private Limited a Rural IT Company based out of Yadamari Mandal in Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh has been awarded the contract of developing and maintaining the IT technologies for the prestigious Smart Village Smart Ward Towards Smart Andhra Pradesh program. The contract involves developing the information portal (www.smart.ap.gov.in) , analytics and reporting engine, registration form, survey data entry module, grading mechanism and the necessary mobile application with geo-tagging technology. A Rural IT company competed with its technical credentials and won the tender against bigger urban companies. “ If Facebook can be made out of a college, we will prove that a world class product can also be made from rural areas of India. This project is one such stepping stone and proof. We are working on a mass scale product that will be useful for farming community across the globe and that day is very near” says Mr. Praveen Nallapothula, Director of Narayani GhramIT Systems.
