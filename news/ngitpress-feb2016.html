NGIT Signs Nation Wide Contract with CSC E-Governance Services India

February 2016


Narayani GhramIT Systems, a Rural IT Park from Chittoor, Andhra Pradesh signs a Nation Wide contract with CSC E-Governance Services India Limited to provide KisanPoint Services to Farmers across India. CSC E-Governance Services India has more than 1.5 lakh Rural Digital Outlets to provide many online services in the areas of G2C and B2C services to public in rural areas of India. With this, Farmers can now go to the nearest CSC center and upload their farm produce details, search for best practices/used equipment and place orders of Inputs/Implements/Farm Machinery Online. NGIT will benefit from using the Nation’s Largest Digital Services Network to deliver its KisanPoint services to bottom of the pyramid society of this Nation – Farmers. The aim is to bring complete transparency in between all stake hoders of agri community and benefit the farmer in reducing the inputs cost and increasing their farm produce sale price.
