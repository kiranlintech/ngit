Last Mile Training :
The national average of students who are having employable skills today is about 8%. Most students when they come out of their degrees do not have employable skills. It takes more than 1 year for these students to acquire these skills spending a lot of money and moving to nearby cities. NGIT has designed an excellent course to make these students employable by providing training by industry experts in their home town. This provides an opportunity to become employable without spending a lot of time and money. In this Internet world, students can also interact with potential employers through web by staying in their home towns and attend personal interviews. Our trained HR staff will help these students seek employment with potential clients while they are on the course work by helping them preparing resumes, assisting them to apply using different job portals etc.

Students from B.Tech-IT, CS, ECE, MCA backgrounds can enrol in the last mile training to get industry standard training and acquire employable skills. Training will focus on Softskills ( Oral Communication, Written Communication, Email Etiquette, Task Management, Time Management and Organizational Behaviour ), Operating System Fundamentals on Red Hat Linux, Domain Skill Training in Mobile Telecom and Internet, Process (All phases of Software Development Life Cycle(SDLC) like Design, Coding, Component Testing, Reviews/Inspections, System Testing, Performance Testing) and Live Project in developing a Web Based Application (using Java/JSP/Servlets/PHP/Android – Mobile Applications). The course duration is 3 months and will provide all the tools to get a job in software industry.
